TAIFEX Night Flow Collector

This is a personal, noncommercial research project for collecting and validating publicly available Taiwan Futures Exchange (TAIFEX) after-hours institutional futures trading reports.

The collector records observation timestamps, source responses, validation results, and historical snapshots to support reproducible quantitative research. It does not provide trading recommendations and is not affiliated with TAIFEX or Google.

Google Drive integration

The project owner uses Google OAuth 2.0 and rclone to save research output in the owner's Google Drive. Google authorization is not required for visitors to this website; this website does not offer public account registration.

The OAuth scope requested for this workflow may allow access to the owner's broader Google Drive, although the automation is configured to operate in a specified research folder. See our privacy policy.
